Price-aware laboratory panels for 9-year mortality risk

Research tool accompanying the manuscript. Not a medical device and not medical advice. Models fitted on NHANES 1999–2010 (adults aged 40–79; 16,153 people, 2,081 deaths). Everything runs in this page; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

How it works

Start with free clinical information. After each step the page estimates 9-year risk and orders the next test only while that risk lies in the printed interval, where finishing the tests is worth their price (value V per correctly flagged death; decision threshold 10% 9-year risk). Order: CMP ($19.90), then CBC ($27.67 in total), then urine albumin/creatinine ratio ($38.63 in total), US Medicare 2026.

Before using it elsewhere

Units are US conventional units, as listed beside each field (creatinine IDMS-standardised; granulocytes = neutrophils + eosinophils + basophils). Missing results: leave the field empty. The page estimates each missing value from the results entered (its conditional mean in the training data) and shows an 80% range for the risk; if the range crosses the decision threshold, it names the tests to measure. Age and sex are always required. Calibration: where the observed-to-expected ratio of deaths is near 1, use the models unchanged; where it is clearly off (about 0.85 in the UK), recalibrate on about 1000 local people with known outcomes.
